Debug controls

  • Press '`' (backtick) to toggle between debug and release mode.
  • Press 'F' to toggle flying. While flying, you do not collide with walls or other entities.
  • Press Backspace to reset checkpoints.
  • Press 'L' or F9 to load a level.
  • Press Esc or F5 to save a level.
  • Press 'Q' and 'E' to switch between types.
  • Left click to place a tile.
  • Right click to replace a tile with air.
  • Click outside the bounds of the level to expand it.
  • Middle click a border row or column to remove it.
  • Press 'R' to switch into entity mode.
  • In entity mode, left click to place an entity seed of the displayed entity.
  • Right click a seed to remove it.
  • Middle click a seed to spawn its entity.
